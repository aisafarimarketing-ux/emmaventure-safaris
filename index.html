Common questions

Asked and answered

Marked up as FAQ data so answer engines can quote it directly.

Who is Emmaventure Safaris?
Emmaventure Safaris is a Tanzania-based tour operator founded by Emmanuel, who grew up at the foot of Mount Kilimanjaro and has guided in Northern Tanzania for over ten years, and his wife Dewi, who is originally from the Netherlands. They personally host the private safaris they design.
Where does Emmaventure Safaris operate?
Northern Tanzania — the Serengeti, Ngorongoro Crater, Tarangire and the Ndutu area — plus Mount Kilimanjaro treks and Zanzibar beach extensions. The company is based in Arusha.
What does a safari with Emmaventure cost?
The Small Group Luxury Safari is a fixed-departure trip at USD 6,900 per person for 7 days. Private tailor-made journeys are quoted individually, because cost depends on season, camps and group size.
Are the safaris private or group?
Most journeys are private and personally hosted. The Small Group Luxury Safari is the exception: a fixed-date, guaranteed-departure trip you can join.
When is the best time to see the Great Migration?
January to March for the Ndutu calving season in the southern Serengeti, and July to October for the river crossings in the north. Emmaventure runs a dedicated journey for each.
Does Emmaventure arrange Kilimanjaro climbs?
Yes. They run the 7-day Machame Route and the 8-day Lemosho Route. Emmanuel has worked as a Kilimanjaro assistant guide.
